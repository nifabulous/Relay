# India and Sri Lanka SSI wave 10k (batch 3)

This batch adds seven unique, canonical BIC-only routes for the IndusInd Bank
IFSC Banking Unit (Gift City). The official SSI PDF is dated 10 June 2025 and
identifies the beneficiary BIC as `INDBINAA`. Its EUR row printed the legacy
`SCBLDEFXXXX` alias, so that row is intentionally omitted in keeping with the
repository's canonical `SCBLDEFFXXX` identity.

| Beneficiary | Source | As of | Unique rows |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| IndusInd Bank Limited, IFSC Banking Unit (`INDBINAAXXX`) | [IndusInd Gift City SSI PDF](https://bank.indusind.bank.in/content/dam/indusind-corporate/Other/financial-institutions/ssi-ibu-unit-IBU-effective31-May-2025.pdf) | 2025-06-10 | 7 |

Rows contain no account or charge fields and remain `unverified`,
`bic_only=true`, and non-routable until operations confirms current settlement
instructions.
